Why Scottsdale chose Dr. Scottsdale

Choosing a plastic surgeon is one of the highest-stakes medical decisions a patient makes. Three credentials matter more than anything advertised on a billboard. First, American Board of Plastic Surgery (ABPS) certification — the only board recognized by the American Board of Medical Specialties for plastic surgery. Many doctors who advertise "cosmetic surgery" in the Valley are board-certified in other specialties (or not certified at all). Dr. Mata is ABPS-certified — verify it directly on CertificationMatters.org.

Second, peer recognition. Dr. Scottsdale® has held Castle Connolly Top Doctor status continuously since 2018 — peer-nominated and credentialed-reviewed, cannot be purchased. He has been named to Newsweek's America's Best Plastic Surgeons, recognized as a RealSelf Top Doctor based on verified patient feedback, and approved by AZ Top Docs for 2025.

Third, surgical volume. 25,000+ procedures across 15+ years of practice is a track record that translates directly into intra-operative judgment. High-volume surgeons see anatomical variation that lower-volume surgeons don't, and that experience shapes everything from incision planning to post-op management.
