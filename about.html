Training

Dr. Mata completed his plastic surgery training across some of the most respected institutions in American medicine — the University of Texas Medical Branch, Saint Joseph Medical Center, and the MD Anderson Cancer Center in Houston, Texas, followed by training at Harvard's Brigham and Women's Hospital in Boston. He then completed an aesthetic surgery fellowship in Miami, Florida, focused exclusively on cosmetic technique.

That breadth of training — reconstructive cancer surgery alongside high-volume aesthetic work — shaped a surgical philosophy that prioritizes both safety and artistry. The Scottsdale Skinny® and Gladiator® were both developed out of the conviction that comprehensive single-session contouring, properly planned, is safer and more aesthetic than serial staged operations.

Practice

In 2017 Dr. Mata founded Natural Results Plastic Surgery in North Scottsdale. The practice serves Scottsdale, Phoenix, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa, and the broader Valley — plus a substantial out-of-state and international patient base that travels in for the trademarked signature procedures. The accredited operating suite is on-site at 7930 E Thompson Peak Parkway.

The practice philosophy is "be your own kind of beautiful" — every consultation begins with the patient's individual goals and anatomy, not a one-size-fits-all aesthetic. That approach has built a 25,000-procedure track record and one of the largest social-media followings in plastic surgery.

Signature procedures

Scottsdale Skinny® — a registered single-session body sculpt combining Vaser-assisted lipo 360, targeted fat transfer, and high-definition muscle etching. It replaces what other surgeons typically stage across 2–3 operations.

Gladiator® Male Makeover — Dr. Scottsdale's comprehensive male body sculpt: high-def lipo of the abdomen, obliques, chest, and flanks, with optional enhancement components.

Magic Shot® — non-surgical male enhancement via in-office injectable. No anesthesia, no downtime, immediate result.
