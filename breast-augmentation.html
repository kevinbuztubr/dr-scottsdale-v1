What it is

Rapid Recovery Breast Augmentation Scottsdale, by Dr. Carlos Mata (a.k.a. Dr. Scottsdale®), uses sophisticated numbing techniques that may expedite healing by numbing the nerves, incision areas, and entire breasts. This breast enhancement surgical method significantly reduces postoperative pain compared to traditional breast augmentation, allowing for a more comfortable recovery. It is recommended that patients refrain from exercise for at least six weeks. The rapid recovery breast augmentation procedure is performed in our sterile surgical operating room.

Implant options

Saline Implants. Silicone shells filled with sterile saline solution. In case of any possible leakage, the body can harmlessly absorb the solution. Implant sizes range from 100cc to 800cc. Best candidates are women with average-sized breasts who want to add volume or improve symmetry. Must be at least 18 years old.

Silicone Implants. Silicone gel breast implants are silicone shells prefilled with silicone gel, available in different sizes, shapes, and textures. Dr. Carlos Mata only uses smooth implants. Sizes range from 100cc to 800cc in different profiles: high, moderate, and low. The profile is chosen depending on the patient's goals. Best candidates are women over 21 years old looking to add volume, cleavage, and/or side projection.

Fat Transfer. For candidates who desire fuller breasts but do not want an artificial implant. Fat transfer to the breast is a natural alternative.

Recovery

The sophisticated numbing technique significantly reduces postoperative pain compared to traditional breast augmentation. Patients are typically more comfortable than they would be after a standard procedure. Driving is permitted only once you are off prescription pain medication and have been cleared by Dr. Mata. It is recommended that patients refrain from exercise for at least six weeks after surgery.
