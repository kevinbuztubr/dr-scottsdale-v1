What it is

The Gladiator® is Dr. Scottsdale's signature male transformation — a procedure built specifically around the male torso and the aesthetic priorities men actually care about: a defined chest, a tight waist, sculpted flanks, and visible abdominal definition. It can be performed standalone for body sculpting alone, or combined with male enhancement procedures in a single surgical plan.

Why men-specific matters

Standard liposuction protocols are calibrated for female anatomy. The Gladiator® uses different fat removal patterns, different muscle-etching geometries, and a different approach to chest tissue management — designed around how male bodies actually store fat and how male physique aesthetics read. The result looks like an aggressive transformation, not a softened one.

What's included

  • Vaser-assisted high-definition liposuction — chest, abdomen, obliques, flanks, lower back
  • Muscle etching — abdominal six-pack carving, oblique lines, pectoral definition
  • Optional: gynecomastia correction via Dr. Scottsdale's One-Stitch technique (single small incision, minimal scarring)
  • Optional: Magic Shot® male enhancement injectable, performed concurrently or staged
  • Custom compression garments + post-op support

How it works

Dr. Scottsdale uses Vaser ultrasound energy to break up fat across the torso before suction — gentler on tissue, more aggressive on fat. He then deliberately sculpts along the natural lines of the abdominal muscles to create a visible athletic look. For patients with gynecomastia, the One-Stitch technique removes glandular tissue through a tiny periareolar incision leaving minimal visible scar.

Recovery

Most patients return to desk work at 1–2 weeks. Light cardio at 3 weeks. Full lifting and impact at 6 weeks. Compression garments are worn continuously the first 6 weeks. Final contour settles at 3–6 months as swelling fully resolves.

Who it's for

  • Men at or near their goal weight wanting visible aesthetic transformation
  • Patients with stubborn flanks, chest, or abdomen that diet/exercise alone can't fix
  • Men dealing with gynecomastia who want a definitive surgical solution
  • Anyone seeking a comprehensive male procedure from a surgeon with male-specific volume and expertise
