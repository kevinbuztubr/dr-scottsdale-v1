What it is

Magic Shot® is Dr. Scottsdale's signature non-surgical penile enhancement using FDA-approved hyaluronic-acid (or biostimulator) fillers. The fillers add girth and overall size through carefully placed injections — the same technique class used safely in aesthetic medicine for decades, applied here by an experienced plastic surgeon.

Why it works

Dermal fillers are safe, predictable, and reversible — three things surgical alternatives are not. The same filler types used worldwide for facial volume restoration are used here, dosed and placed specifically for this indication. There's no foreign material, no implant, no anesthesia recovery, no scar.

What's included

  • Filler-type selection during your consultation — Juvederm-family, Radiesse, or biostimulator depending on goals
  • Topical numbing + local anesthetic block — virtually painless
  • In-office injection by Dr. Scottsdale (not a tech, not a delegate)
  • Up to a 20% girth increase typical
  • Immediate visible result
  • Follow-up visit included for any touch-ups

Recovery

Most patients return to normal activity immediately. Some bruising, redness, or mild swelling for 1–3 days is normal. Sexual activity can typically resume after 1–2 weeks per Dr. Scottsdale's guidance. No work downtime required.

How long it lasts

Result duration depends on filler choice + individual metabolism + activity level. Hyaluronic acid fillers (Juvederm-family) typically last 6 months to 2 years. Calcium-hydroxylapatite fillers (Radiesse) tend to last on the longer end of that range. Biostimulator fillers can last 2–3 years. Patients can maintain results indefinitely with periodic touch-ups.

Who it's for

  • Men seeking enhancement without surgery, anesthesia, or scars
  • Anyone wanting a reversible, adjustable, low-commitment option
  • Patients who want to try enhancement before committing to a surgical solution
  • Healthy adults without contraindications to dermal fillers

Discretion + privacy

Dr. Scottsdale runs a discreet, male-focused practice. Consultations and procedures are scheduled to maximize privacy. The procedure itself is performed in a private treatment suite — no waiting room handoffs, no signage, no staff besides Dr. Scottsdale and one assistant.
